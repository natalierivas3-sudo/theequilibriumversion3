# The Equilibrium: version history

Major versions only. Each one is its own site, so they can be compared side by side.

## Version 3 (this repository)
Live: https://natalierivas3-sudo.github.io/theequilibriumversion3/

- Home opens with nothing selected; Guide me, Surprise me, and Calm me are centered and open only when picked.
- Calm me: five choices up front (Set it aside, Rant, 3 good things, Other coping skills, an 8-minute trauma-informed guided video) and a 3-question check-in that starts with "How regulated do you feel?" (1–5). At 5 it shows crisis support first, then a breathing guide.
- New Coping skills page: 10 quick tools adapted from DBT, a 0–10 rating before and after each exercise, "My kit", and a crisis bar pinned to the screen.
- "Find a study spot" shows the campus map above the results and sorts spots closest first; coffee shops and Austin Central Library are on the map.
- Search understands everyday words ("stressed", "therapy", "dogs"); Resources has category buttons; Insight Timer is listed as a free meditation app.
- Accessibility: all pages pass WCAG 2 AA automated checks (darker text colors, larger labels).
- Link previews (title, description, image) for sharing; Tip of the day on Home; student quotes removed from cards.

## Version 2 (Test version 57)
Live: https://natalierivas3-sudo.github.io/equilibrium-test/

- Simplified Home: the question, three buttons, and six choices; results appear only after picking one.
- Study spots, Resources, UT maps, and crisis support each on their own page instead of one long scroll.
- Cleaner, consistent card buttons; UT maps cards with short tags; Origin story with an "In short" summary.
- Guide me is open when the page loads.

## Version 1 (original)
Live: https://natalierivas3-sudo.github.io/the-equilibrium/

- The first site: everything on one long page.

- v3 update (Nov 25): tapping a plain "988" link now asks Call 988 or Text 988 first. Buttons already labeled "Call 988" or "Text 988" go straight through.
